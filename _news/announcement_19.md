7 papers accepted and will be presented at INTERSPEECH 2025! The topics include [speech datasets for science](https://www.isca-archive.org/interspeech_2025/shi25g_interspeech.html), [child speech classification](https://www.isca-archive.org/interspeech_2025/feng25b_interspeech.pdf), [child speech-LLMs](https://www.isca-archive.org/interspeech_2025/xu25g_interspeech.pdf), [child speech ASR](https://www.isca-archive.org/interspeech_2025/shi25h_interspeech.pdf), [multimodal child behavior understanding](https://www.isca-archive.org/interspeech_2025/kommineni25_interspeech.pdf), [categorized speech emotion recognition](https://www.isca-archive.org/interspeech_2025/feng25_interspeech.pdf), and [arousal-valence speech recognition](https://www.isca-archive.org/interspeech_2025/lertpetchpun25_interspeech.pdf).
